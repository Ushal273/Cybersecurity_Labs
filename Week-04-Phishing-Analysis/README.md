# Week 4 – Phishing Analysis

## Objective
Learn how to identify and analyse phishing emails.

---

## What is Phishing?
Phishing is a cyber attack where criminals send fake emails to trick
people into revealing passwords, clicking malicious links, or
downloading malware.

---

## Types of Phishing

- Phishing – Mass fake emails sent to many people
- Spear Phishing – Targeted attack on a specific person
- Whaling – Targeted attack on executives like CEOs
- Smishing – Phishing via SMS
- Vishing – Phishing via phone call

---

## What to Look For in a Suspicious Email

- Sender email address does not match the real company
- Suspicious or mismatched links
- Urgent language pressuring the recipient to act fast
- Spelling and grammar mistakes
- Unexpected attachments

---

## Key Email Header Fields

- From – who sent the email
- Reply-To – where replies go (often different in phishing)
- Received – the path the email travelled
- X-Originating-IP – the real IP address of the sender

---

## Key Takeaways
Phishing is one of the most common attack vectors used by
cybercriminals. Analysing email headers and understanding red flags
is a critical skill for any SOC analyst.
